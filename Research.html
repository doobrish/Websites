Research

Research Page

The research page provides a detailed summary of the methodology chosen in the process of designing and developing Trendzy web site. Before the design process began, a comprehensive research of the available sites had been done to analyze the layout strategies, content organisation, navigation styles, typography, colour scheme and user interaction patterns. This research was not an attempt at duplicating the current platforms but was rather an endeavor to learn the lesson in regards to good design and apply the same to Trendzy with a couple of unique additions and optimisations expected to make the platform more clear, usable and enjoyable to use.

Website References

In order to develop Trendzy, two main reference websites were utilized in order to inform the choice of layout and interface: Jeeve and Lochtree. All these websites provided useful information in the context of certain aspects of Trendzy. Although Jeeve was used as a model of the Shop and Blog page, Lochtree was used as a model of Home Page structure, Navigation bar and footer design mainly. Through an examination of these websites, we managed to retrieve valuable ideas and use them intelligently to the distinctive design of Trendzy.

Jeeve Jeeve is an e-commerce-based and content-driven site, which offers numerous parts containing factual specification of products and informative blog articles. In the course of the research we focused on learning how Jeeve sorts its products, the role of spacing and alignment in increasing the readability and the way users move among the listing of products to their respective product pages. In addition, the blog pages of Jeeve were reviewed on the basis of the layout of the articles, the spacing among paragraphs, typography, as well as the use of headings to enhance reliability of reading. This was aimed at determining design patterns that would enable the user to understand what a design has to offer without flooding the visitor and, determining how Jeeve manages to retain a lot of information at the same time maintaining a business-like-organized look.

On the other hand, Lochtree was researched to serve as the design inspiration to the Trendzy Home Page, Navigation Bar and footer. As shown, Lochtree has a lot of clean typography and effective navigation choices that make the interface look minimal and at the same time is very professional and well-organized. The simplicity of its navigation bar allows people to navigate to the most essential features without becoming lost, and the critical information and links are discussed in the footer in a rather compact format. Viewing Lochtree helped us to see how such a small site can be used to create a site that is easy to use by first visitors and allows it still to be considered professional by regular users.

By the synthesis of the insights produced by both Jeeve and Lochtree, we could even identify how the current web platforms have come to place more importance on various components of usability. Jeeve taught about the richness of content, organization, and structuring of voluminous information, but Lochtree depicted how lean and smallness of message and clarity could contribute to the user experience. In the research, we were able to determine efficacious design elements and those that may potentially add uncertainties or complexities to the users.

Similarities

Trendzy has several similarities with the reference websites. Similar to the two Jeeve and Lochtree, Trendzy still utilizes a top-positioned navigation bar that is available throughout the site, thus giving persons easy access to the key areas of the site. This designing option means that users will have an easy time in understanding the format of the site in their initial view or even when they pay another visit to the site. The navigation menu is logical and well organized and inspired, specifically, by the minimalism of Tochtree.

One more similarity is the constant usage of typography, interspacing, and the use of section structure. Similar to Jeeve, the Shop and Blog pages of trendy are well aligned, leaving enough space and readable fonts and thus the content is easily understood. Repetition of the design elements can be found on the different pages; a sense of unity within the visuals brings about a sense of familiarity, and allows the user to browse the site with ease.

Overall, the application of the design pattern based on the inspiration of Jeeve and Lochtree helps Trendzy to provide the customer with a familiar and reliable user experience and maintain its unique image. Users do not feel lost or overwhelmed by the number of products, blogs, and sections they can go to with ease and find their way around.

Differences

Although there are similarities, a number of differences were directly included in the design of Trendzy. In contrast to Jeeve, which is inclined to present a broad amount of content and several categories of products at the same time, Trendzy should be simple and clear. There is a reduction in congestion of pages and individuals are allowed to focus on important information without being overwhelmed with information due to too many pages. This style puts emphasis on usability and readability as opposed to content density.

Moreover, where Lochtree offers a bare-bone home page, Trendzy elaborates on the same, and deploys the acquired principles in more than one page, always. The colour range is reduced to black and white, providing the sleek effect of modernity, and the other two characters, Jeeve and Lochtree use more colours and underlighting to distinguish the parts visually. These differences enable Trendzy to maintain its unique image and at the same time enjoy the proven design practices.

The other difference is with regards to the incorporation of pictures and screen shots. Trendzy puts an image carefully to facilitate the content without drawing the attention of the user to the point Jeeve has a high number of images in a section. The minimal Lochtree employs a bigger header image and other important graphics to give it emphasis; Trendzy has the right balance between clarity, simplicity, and visual appeal.

Improvements Made

Following the results of Jeeve and Lochtree, some developments were also made in the Trendzy design. Navigation was optimized in the way that the users could navigate through every page with ease and clarity. Important parts were also separated by proper distances, and unwanted details were eliminated in order to have emphasis on necessary contents.

The shop and blog pages have been arranged in a way that they are easy to read and read, which is due to using the same headings, paragraph separation, and the logical flow of the information used in the long layout of Jeeve. Lochtree improved the minimalism style to add to the homepage, the navigation bar and the footer keeping the interface professional and clean without any unnecessary complexity.

All these enhancements will result in a pleasing and performing website. Users can easily navigate, browse products, and read blog articles, and the interface is also up-to-date,clean, and uncluttered.

Conclusion

We have been able to derive major design concepts of Trendzy through an in-depth study of Jeeve and Tochtree. The design of the areas of the Shop and Blog said Jeeve, and he shared his experience with the structure of the contents and their readability, whereas the home page, and the design of the navigation bar and footer were inspired by Lochtree. The combination of these sources and the original identity of Trendzy helped us to develop a balanced user-friendly interface which was clear and understandable.

The originality and innovative Trendzy web site is the reflection of the combination of inspiration and creativity. It simplifies layouts and minimizes visual clutter, and few design strategies are proven, which makes it easier to integrate, besides considering user understanding. The use of this methodology ensures easy navigation, easy access to information and smooth and fluent browsing experience on all the devices.
